How BelieversMeet Works

BelieversMeet brings church communities and their members into one place to discover, connect, grow in faith and serve together.

Create an Account

For individuals and members

Your journey in four steps

Start with a church community, then use the features available to your account and membership.

1

Discover a church

Browse church profiles, learn about service schedules, programmes and leadership, and find a community you would like to connect with.

2

Create an account

Register or sign in. You can request to connect with a church; membership and access to church-specific areas follow the church's approval and account rules.

3

Participate and grow

Join community conversations, share prayer requests and testimonies, follow sermons and broadcasts, and take part in groups, events and programmes.

4

Connect and serve

Message other members, start an invited one-to-one video call from a member conversation, discover serving opportunities, request pastoral counselling and support your church through giving.

Singles Corner

Meet people through church-hosted gatherings

The current Singles Corner offering is represented by the Singles Meetup category in Events. Browse church-hosted meetups and gatherings, then follow the event's own registration information.

This describes the available Singles Meetup event category; it does not imply a separate dating, matching or private matchmaking service.

A note about access and calls

Access follows your account

Some pages are public, while member features require sign-in. Church administration, moderation and financial pages are limited to accounts with the relevant role and permissions. A link in the menu does not replace the page's access checks.

Video calls use invitations

One-to-one video calls are initiated from a member conversation using a call invitation. The invited participant must join the call, and the browser will ask for camera and microphone access.
